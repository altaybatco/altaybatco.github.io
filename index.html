🌱 Nile Delta Agricultural Heritage

Rooted in Egyptian Soil, Engineered for Global Trust

ALTAYBAT FOR EXPORT (الطيبات للتصدير) is an established Egyptian agricultural commodity enterprise situated in the heart of Egypt's richest alluvial farmland across the Nile Delta and Upper Egypt. For decades, our agrarian partnerships with select farming estates have guaranteed high-germination, pesticide-controlled crops meeting rigid European and GCC food security regulations.

Our state-of-the-art sorting, grading, and packaging infrastructure utilizes multi-spectral optical Sortex machinery to isolate foreign materials, discolored seeds, and splits with 99.5% accuracy. Every shipment and container packed carries the seal of authentic Egyptian agricultural mastery.

Our Seed-to-Container Quality Journey

1

Agronomic Selection

Direct farm contracts, regulated seed genetics, soil mineral testing, and zero-residue irrigation monitoring.

2

Optical Sortex Grading

Mechanical de-stoning, aspiration, vibration screens, and dual-camera optical Sortex color sorting up to 99.5% purity.

3

Laboratory Assay

Stringent testing for moisture content (≤14%), aflatoxins, pesticide residue limits, and caliber distribution verification.

4

Export Bagging & Packing

Automated electronic weighing into 25kg & 50kg standard export bags and 1000kg bulk jumbo bags with reinforced double stitching.

5

Fumigation & Port Dispatch

Container palletization, kraft paper lining, certified aluminum phosphide fumigation, and direct ocean dispatch from Alexandria or Damietta.
